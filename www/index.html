Webserv - Validation Suite

1. GET & Cookies (Session Management)

Tests the transmission of HTTP_COOKIE and the reception of Set-Cookie.

2. POST Multipart & Upload (Image Display)

Tests CONTENT_TYPE, CONTENT_LENGTH, and reading binary data from stdin.

3. DELETE (Static & CGI)

Tests the handling of REQUEST_METHOD=DELETE.

4. CGI Responses (The 4 Types)

Tests CGI response routing via QUERY_STRING.

  • Document Response (Standard)
  • Local Redirect (Server intercepts)
  • Client Redirect (Browser redirects)
  • Client Redirect with Document
